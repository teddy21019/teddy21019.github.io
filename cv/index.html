Professional Summary

My research interests lie in the intersection of production networks and international economics. Specifically, I am interested in how firms respond to trade policies and adjust their decisions regarding linkages, and conversely, how linkages influence the propagation of shocks. Additionally, I am interested in the econometric modeling of these models.
